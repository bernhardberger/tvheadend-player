Channels Grid is now a wall of cards without program info, so you see more channels at once. Progress bars are orange for what you're watching, cyan for other programs and red for running recordings, and program info shows the minutes left. Recordings you deleted no longer show as failed. Recordings get markers at commercial breaks and cuts, and from chapters in MKV and MP4 files. All changes: github.com/bernhardberger/tvheadend-player/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
