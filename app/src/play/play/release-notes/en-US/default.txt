Apps that show what's playing on your TV, like Home Assistant, now get the channel, program and artwork, and Next/Previous from media controls switch channels. Recordings mark program start and end with flags, and OK on a running recording offers playing it first. During the testing phase, Home shows "What's new in 0.4.13" next to the clock.
