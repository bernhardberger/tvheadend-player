Recordings now show ad breaks, cuts, chapters and the program start and end on the timeline. Up from the seekbar opens the marks with their names; OK jumps there. When an ad break starts, a Skip button counts down for 10 seconds. Settings > Playback > Recording marks can skip breaks automatically, skip cuts and choose where playback continues. Back in Settings returns to the row you came from. All changes: github.com/bernhardberger/tvheadend-player/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
